Answer or dismiss a question raised by one of your subagent sessions.

You receive a `<question_request>` block when a session you started blocks on a
question. That child is stopped until somebody responds. Use this tool to
respond in its place when the decision is yours to make.

- `requestID`: the id from the `<question_request id="...">` block.
- `action`: `"answer"` to supply answers, `"reject"` to dismiss the question so
  the child continues without one.
- `answers`: required when `action` is `"answer"`. One array per question, in
  the order the questions were listed, each holding the labels you choose.
  Questions marked `multiple="true"` accept more than one label; questions that
  allow a custom answer accept free text instead of a listed label.

You may only respond to a question raised by a direct child of your own
session. If the decision genuinely belongs to a human, do not call this tool:
leave the request pending, say so in your reply, and a human can still answer
it directly.
